## The rules

- Name ordinary, everyday food. "Grilled chicken with rice and salad" is the
  register. Not a recipe, not a diet, not a programme.
- **No quantities of any kind.** No grams, no calories, no portions, no
  macronutrients, no "high protein", no "low carb". You are naming what is on
  the plate, not prescribing what is in it.
- **No health or clinical claims.** Nothing about weight, blood sugar, recovery,
  metabolism, deficiency, or what a food does to a body. If a sentence would
  need a dietitian behind it, it does not belong here.
- Each meal is a `name` and a `kind`. `kind` is one of `breakfast`, `lunch`,
  `dinner`, `snack`. Give the day a sensible shape — one breakfast, one lunch,
  one dinner, and snacks after that.
- A name is a short phrase, not a sentence, and never ends with a full stop.
- Vary the day. The same protein in every meal is not a day's food.
